Thinker

Diagnosis before action.

Thinker is a governed issue-resolution product for teams that need evidence, not guesses. It records the question, safe answer, evidence, resolution plan, and audit trail.

  • Read-first diagnosis with RAG and eligible read-action evidence
  • Persisted sessions, evidence items, plans, and exports
  • Partner-safe redaction for assigned-store support
  • Available as a controlled capability in the current commerce path

Resolver

Governed resolution when follow-up is approved.

Resolver turns a Thinker diagnosis into a controlled proposal, policy decision, dry-run, confirmation, and execution record. It is not broad autonomous automation.

  • Proposal and policy ledger
  • Non-mutating dry-run before real execution
  • Exact confirmation and idempotency controls
  • Current governed execution scope: support escalation records
1. ProposalConnected to Thinker evidence
2. PolicyAllowed only when assignment and evidence pass
3. Dry-runExpected side effects and rollback posture
4. ConfirmExact confirmation text and idempotency key
5. ExecuteAudited low-risk support workflow
