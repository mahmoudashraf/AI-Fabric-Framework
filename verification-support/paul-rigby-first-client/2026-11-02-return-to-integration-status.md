# First-Customer Integration Status

Date: 2 November 2026

## Decision

The owner accepted the remaining Support semantic limitations and requested the
qualified changes in production, then a return to 010.55. Stop the optimization
stream; do not turn the remaining model mistakes into another framework release.
LoomAI customer Support and the Northfield dealership demo are separate products
and deployments. Support qualification does not close the dealership pilot.

**010.55 is not complete, and we are not waiting only on owner inputs.** It is
reasonable to approach the prospect now with the explicitly fictional demo and
discovery proposal. A real dealer production integration is not yet qualified.
