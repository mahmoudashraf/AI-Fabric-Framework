## Scope

Configurable bounded repairs of internal intent/source decisions followed by one
context/history answer for ordinary chat. No domain-selection question, hidden
tool retry, source-permission expansion, confirmation bypass or write replay.
Provider outages, actual access failures and strict Task contracts remain distinct.

Advanced -> Recovery policy reuses the shared provider editor. The two persisted
limits are `maxRepairRounds` (default3, range0-10) and
`repairAdmissionTimeoutMs` (default30000, range1000-300000). The window admits new
repairs; it is not cancellation of an in-flight provider call or a hard total
request deadline. Existing extraction/provider/funding limits remain effective.
