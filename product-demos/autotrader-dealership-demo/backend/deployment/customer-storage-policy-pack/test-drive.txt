NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - TEST-DRIVE REQUIREMENTS AND REQUESTS
Policy version: 1.1
Effective date: 1 November 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is fictional demonstration content, not a real dealership commitment.

ELIGIBILITY AND IDENTIFICATION
The driver must be at least 21 years old and present an original full UK or EU
driving licence held for at least 12 months. The team may request a second proof
of address if the licence address is not current. Only the named, verified
driver may drive. An additional driver requires approval and the same checks.

DURATION AND ADVANCE BOOKING
A normal demonstration lasts up to 30 minutes, follows an agreed route and is
accompanied by a Northfield team member. Thirty minutes describes the driving
duration, not the advance-booking interval.

Request a test drive in advance. This policy does not specify a minimum number
of minutes, hours or days of notice. Vehicle and staff availability determine
whether the requested time can be accepted.

REQUEST DETAILS AND CONFIRMATION
A request needs the customer's name, email address, phone number and selected
vehicle. The customer may also supply a preferred date/time and accessibility
or child-seat needs. Explain any additional sign-in requirement shown by the
connected request form; the assistant cannot bypass that requirement.

The customer explicitly confirms submission. Asking what is required, opening
a form or cancelling it does not submit a request. A submitted request is not
a confirmed appointment. The dealership checks the vehicle and staff
availability and sends the actual appointment confirmation separately.
