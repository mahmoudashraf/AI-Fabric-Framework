NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - DELIVERY, COLLECTION AND HANDOVER
Policy version: 1.1
Effective date: 1 November 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is fictional demonstration content. It is not a real dealership promise,
Auto Trader policy or legal advice.

LOCAL DELIVERY
Standard local delivery within 25 miles of Northfield Riverside costs GBP 49.
Allow three working days after cleared payment and vehicle preparation. The
team must agree the delivery slot; an enquiry is not a confirmed delivery.

Delivery from 26 to 75 miles is quoted individually. This policy does not give
an exact charge for a home 40 miles away. Delivery beyond 75 miles is not
offered by this demonstration policy. Do not treat the GBP 49 local price as
the price for an address outside the 25-mile area.

BEFORE COLLECTION OR DELIVERY
The team agrees an appointment and provides the final balance statement.
Cleared funds, identity verification, vehicle preparation and insurance
effective from handover must be complete. The customer brings photo
identification, evidence of insurance and any agreed part-exchange keys and
documents. A delivery also needs a completed remote condition review.

AT HANDOVER
The adviser checks the vehicle registration, recorded mileage, agreed
accessories, charging cable where applicable, keys, supplied service history
and visible condition with the customer. The customer receives a handover
summary and the dealership's aftercare contact route.

If payment, identity or insurance evidence is incomplete, the team pauses the
handover and arranges a new slot after the missing requirement is resolved.
The assistant cannot waive those checks or confirm that a slot is booked.
