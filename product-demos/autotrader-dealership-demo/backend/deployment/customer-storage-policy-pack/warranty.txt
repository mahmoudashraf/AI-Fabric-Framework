NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - USED-VEHICLE WARRANTY AND AFTERCARE
Policy version: 1.1
Effective date: 1 November 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is fictional demonstration content, not legal advice or a real warranty.

Qualifying used vehicles include a 90-day demonstration mechanical warranty
covering the engine and gearbox. Wear items and accidental damage are excluded.
Contact the dealership before authorising repairs so the team can check the
vehicle's coverage and explain the approved repair process.

The vehicle-specific warranty schedule determines whether a vehicle qualifies
and records any additional cover. This general policy does not establish
traction-battery cover, electric-drive component cover, a manufacturer's
remaining warranty, a monetary claim limit or a guaranteed reimbursement.
Ask the team to verify the relevant schedule when those facts are absent.

Give the team the vehicle reference, reported fault, when it started and a
safe contact route. Do not promise approval or begin an unauthorised repair.
This demonstration policy does not determine a customer's statutory rights.
