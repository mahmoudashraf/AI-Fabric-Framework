NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - TEST DRIVE REQUIREMENTS
Document version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is a fictional policy used only in the LoomAI dealership demonstration.

The driver must be at least 21 years old and must present an original full UK
or EU driving licence held for at least 12 months. Northfield may request a
second proof of address when the licence address is not current.

A test-drive request needs the customer's name, email address, phone number,
the selected vehicle and, when available, a preferred date or time. The
customer must explicitly confirm submission. The request is not a booking
until a dealership team member checks vehicle and staff availability and sends
a confirmed appointment.

Only the named, verified driver may drive. Normal demonstrations are
accompanied by a Northfield team member, use an agreed route and last up to 45
minutes. Additional drivers must meet the same requirements and be approved in
advance. Accessibility or child-seat needs should be mentioned when requesting
the appointment so the team can prepare safely.
