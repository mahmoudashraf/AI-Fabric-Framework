NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - CUSTOMER CARE AND COMPLAINTS POLICY
Document version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is a fictional policy used only in the LoomAI dealership demonstration.

Customers can raise a concern with Northfield customer care by email, phone or
at the Northfield Riverside reception. Include the customer's name, preferred
contact method, vehicle registration or stock reference when relevant, the
date of the issue and the outcome requested. Do not include payment-card data
or identity-document images in an ordinary chat message.

Northfield acknowledges a complaint within two working days. A named case
owner aims to provide a substantive response within 10 working days. If more
time is needed, the case owner explains why and gives a new response date.

A customer who remains dissatisfied may request review by the customer-care
manager. A complaint submitted through the assistant creates only a contact
request after explicit confirmation; it does not record a formal resolution or
promise compensation.
