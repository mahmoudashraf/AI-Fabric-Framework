NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - USED VEHICLE WARRANTY AND AFTERCARE POLICY
Document version: 2.0
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is a fictional policy used only in the LoomAI dealership demonstration.

STANDARD COVER

Every qualifying used vehicle sold directly by Northfield Motor House includes
a limited warranty for 90 days or 3,000 miles, whichever occurs first. Cover
starts when the customer collects the vehicle or the vehicle is delivered.

The standard warranty covers sudden mechanical failure of internally
lubricated engine, gearbox and drivetrain components, plus failure of major
factory-fitted electrical components. A repair must be authorised by
Northfield before work begins.

BATTERY AND MANUFACTURER COVER

The standard warranty covers the 12-volt auxiliary battery for sudden failure
during the first 30 days. Electric-vehicle traction batteries are not given a
new capacity guarantee by this policy. Any remaining manufacturer traction
battery warranty is vehicle-specific and must be checked against the vehicle
listing and manufacturer record.

EXCLUSIONS

Normal wear, routine servicing, tyres, brake friction materials, bulbs,
cosmetic damage, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised modification and
faults disclosed before purchase are excluded. The policy does not replace any
statutory consumer rights.

HOW TO REQUEST AFTERCARE

Contact the Northfield customer-care team before authorising repairs. Provide
the vehicle registration or stock reference, current mileage, a description of
the fault and any warning-light photograph. The team acknowledges a complete
request within one working day and provides the next diagnostic step. An
independent repair invoice is not automatically reimbursable without prior
written authorisation.
