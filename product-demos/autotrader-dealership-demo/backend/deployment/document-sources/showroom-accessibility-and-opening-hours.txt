NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - RIVERSIDE SHOWROOM INFORMATION
Document version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is fictional public information used only in the LoomAI dealership
demonstration.

Northfield Riverside sales opening hours are Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00,
Saturday 09:00 to 17:00 and Sunday 10:00 to 16:00. Public-holiday hours may
differ and should be confirmed with the team before travel.

The customer entrance and sales floor have step-free access. Two accessible
parking bays are beside the main entrance, and an accessible toilet is
available on the ground floor. Customers may request a quiet appointment,
seating during handover or extra time to inspect vehicle controls. Assistance
dogs are welcome.

Vehicle-specific location and availability must be checked from current stock
data because vehicles can move between sites. This document describes the
Riverside visitor facilities; it must not be used to claim that a named vehicle
is currently held there.
