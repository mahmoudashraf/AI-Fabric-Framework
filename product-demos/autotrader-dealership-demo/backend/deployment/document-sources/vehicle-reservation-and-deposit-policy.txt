NORTHFIELD MOTOR HOUSE - VEHICLE RESERVATION AND DEPOSIT POLICY
Document version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 October 2026
Audience: Public demonstration

This is a fictional policy used only in the LoomAI dealership demonstration.

A customer may request a 48-hour reservation on one currently available
vehicle. The reservation amount is GBP 99. A reservation begins only after a
Northfield team member confirms current availability and issues a reservation
receipt. A chat message or callback request does not reserve a vehicle.

The GBP 99 reservation amount is applied to the purchase price if the sale
continues. It is refundable when the customer cancels before the 48-hour hold
expires, when Northfield cannot supply the materially described vehicle, or
when the customer rejects the vehicle at the first arranged inspection or test
drive. Approved refunds return to the original payment method and normally
appear within three to five working days.

At the end of 48 hours an unextended reservation expires and the vehicle may
return to sale. Northfield cannot accept a reservation for a vehicle already
sold, withdrawn, or under an earlier confirmed hold. Extending a hold requires
written confirmation from the dealership team.
