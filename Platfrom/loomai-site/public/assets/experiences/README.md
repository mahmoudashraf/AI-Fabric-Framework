# Business Experience Media

`demo-support-agent.webp` reuses the existing Console product-preview asset at
`Platfrom/ui/public/products/demo-support-agent.webp`. It is a fictional,
AI-generated support-agent portrait, not a claimed employee or available agent.
Its original prompt and provenance remain in that directory's `README.md`.

Vehicle photographs are referenced from the existing `/assets/demos/dealership/`
collection. Fictional stock names, prices and simulator examples do not describe
a real dealership, inventory offer or Auto Trader partnership.

Workspace illustrations are semantic HTML and Lucide icons, not screenshots of
a configured customer account. They contain no guaranteed delivery terms.

`example-forma-chair.webp` and `example-studio-chair.webp` reuse the generated,
fictional Console product photos. Their provenance and prompt descriptions are
in `Platfrom/ui/public/products/README.md`. These are example products only,
not a customer catalogue or live offer.

`google-drive.png` and `slack.svg` reuse the Console's existing product-preview
assets. Google Drive's logo comes from Google's product branding; Slack's logo
comes from Devicon v2.17.0 under the adjacent `devicon-LICENSE.txt`. Original
source URLs and trademark notes are in the Console asset README. These illustrate
external systems, not certified partnerships or preinstalled integrations.

The homepage kit cards reuse `demo-support-agent-headset.webp`,
`example-electric-suv.webp` and `kit-support-team.webp` from
`Platfrom/ui/public/products/`. They are generated fictional examples; their
source descriptions remain in that directory's README. The portrait does not
represent an assigned or available agent.

The locally served Manrope font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License in
`manrope-OFL.txt`. `whatsapp.svg` uses the unmodified WhatsApp icon path from
`@mui/icons-material` under the adjacent `mui-icons-LICENSE.txt`. The small
WhatsApp and mobile symbols illustrate possible Desk channels, not an active
integration or a claimed WhatsApp partnership.
