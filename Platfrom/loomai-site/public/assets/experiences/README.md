# Business Experience Media

`demo-support-agent.webp` reuses the existing Console product-preview asset at
`Platfrom/ui/public/products/demo-support-agent.webp`. It is a fictional,
AI-generated support-agent portrait, not a claimed employee or available agent.
Its original prompt and provenance remain in that directory's `README.md`.

Vehicle photographs are referenced from the existing `/assets/demos/dealership/`
collection. Fictional stock names, prices and simulator examples do not describe
a real dealership, inventory offer or Auto Trader partnership.

Workspace illustrations are semantic HTML and Lucide icons, not screenshots of
a configured customer account. They contain no guaranteed delivery terms.

`example-forma-chair.webp` and `example-studio-chair.webp` reuse the generated,
fictional Console product photos. Their provenance and prompt descriptions are
in `Platfrom/ui/public/products/README.md`. These are example products only,
not a customer catalogue or live offer.
