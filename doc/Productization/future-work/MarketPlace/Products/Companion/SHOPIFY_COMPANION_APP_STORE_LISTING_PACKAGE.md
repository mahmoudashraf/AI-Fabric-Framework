# Shopify Companion App Store Listing Package

Status: builder-mode launch package baseline

This document is the canonical App Store listing package for Shopify Companion.

It exists to keep launch copy honest and aligned with the product that is actually shipped.

## 1. Listing Posture

Shopify Companion should be positioned as:

- embedded store intelligence for Shopify storefronts
- chat-assisted depth when needed
- read-first by default
- governed action depth only when the merchant is on the Elite posture and that capability is live

Do not position it as:

- an autonomous checkout bot
- a generic AI chatbot
- a full support desk replacement
- a broad workflow automation platform

## 2. Primary Listing Copy

### App name

- `Loom Companion`

### Short subtitle options

Use one:

1. `Embedded AI shopping intelligence for Shopify`
2. `AI search, product insights, and policy answers for Shopify`
3. `Storefront AI search and shopper guidance for Shopify`

### One-sentence description

Use one:

1. `Add AI search, product insights, FAQs, comparison help, and grounded policy answers to your Shopify storefront.`
2. `Help shoppers discover products and get grounded answers from your store’s real catalog, content, and policies.`

### Full description baseline

Recommended baseline:

`Loom Companion brings embedded AI store intelligence to Shopify. Give shoppers AI search, product insights, policy answers, FAQs, and comparison guidance powered by your live store data. The app stays read-first by default, supports bounded merchant controls for sync and freshness, and keeps operational visibility in the merchant app and platform admin surfaces.`

## 3. Tier-Safe Product Truth

These are the claims that are currently safe.

### Free

Safe claim:

- `AI search for your storefront with a capped catalog and powered-by posture.`

### Starter

Safe claim:

- `Read-only store intelligence with contextual surfaces, FAQ, comparison, and shopper analytics.`

### Elite

Safe claim only when the live commercial rollout is actually enabled:

- `Governed commerce actions with explicit confirmation and audit trail.`

Do not claim Elite generally unless:

- the live store is actually on the Elite plan
- the merchant can use the governed surfaces
- verification for that posture is clean

## 4. Screenshot Shot List

Minimum screenshot set:

1. Merchant app home showing:
   - store intelligence health
   - launch and App Review readiness
   - tier ladder
2. Merchant storefront preview showing:
   - AI search
   - contextual pill
   - product insight
   - policy strip
   - product FAQ
   - comparison
3. Storefront AI search result state
4. Product page with product insight and policy strip
5. Product page or collection page with contextual pill
6. Product FAQ or comparison surface in use

Optional Elite-only screenshot set:

7. Governed action history in merchant/platform admin
8. Guided commerce surface with explicit confirmation messaging

## 5. Demo And Review Store Rules

The listing/demo store should:

- have a clean theme with the app embed enabled
- have enough products, collections, policies, and content to make the surfaces look real
- avoid unsupported or placeholder surfaces
- avoid showing Elite guided actions unless the store is truly configured for that posture

## 6. Allowed Claims

These are safe:

- `AI search`
- `product insights`
- `policy answers`
- `product FAQ`
- `comparison guidance`
- `grounded answers from store data`
- `embedded storefront intelligence`
- `merchant-ready freshness and support visibility`

## 7. Disallowed Claims

Do not ship listing copy that claims:

- `autonomous purchasing`
- `checkout automation`
- `writes customer or order data automatically`
- `full customer support automation`
- `all review providers supported`
- `all Shopify themes supported with zero setup`
- `Elite actions` when the live commercial rollout is not enabled

## 8. Review-Safe Notes

If reviewers or merchants ask about actions:

- default posture is read-first
- governed actions are a bounded higher-tier capability
- governed actions require confirmation and auditability
- governed actions are not part of the default V1 review story unless the review package explicitly enables them

## 9. Launch Asset Checklist

Before submission or merchant-facing launch:

1. final subtitle chosen from the approved set
2. final long description reviewed against live product truth
3. screenshot set captured from the live product
4. review screencast matches the actual surface set
5. App Review guide, support runbook, and design-partner checklist all agree on product posture
