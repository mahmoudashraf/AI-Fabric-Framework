Title: Connect LoomAI Desk and move between AI and human support
Document ID: loomai-support-11
Product: LoomAI Desk, LoomAI Platform and LoomAI Engage
Audience: Customer owners, registered support agents and workspace users
Reviewed: 2026-10-31

Understand the service and permissions

LoomAI Desk is a shared managed human-support service, not an inbox service or
database created for every deployment. Each deployment target has its own binding
and agent assignments. Multiple apps using that target share its team. Staging
and production assignments are separate. The deployment remains authoritative
for its AI conversation and review execution.

Follow this guide only when your workspace exposes Human support and the relevant
handover controls. A Console owner can manage assignments but is not automatically
a Support agent or Reviewer. Agent registration alone grants no queue access.

Customer owner: enable and assign

1. Open Human support for the owned deployment and select its target release.
   Review unrelated draft edits before selecting Enable LoomAI Desk. The normal
   publish, apply and verification process prepares the target-specific binding.
   Wait for Enabled; merely saving a setting is not successful enablement.
2. If the Engage app still uses an older release, explicitly assign the verified
   release using the existing app assignment controls. Applying a deployment
   update alone does not switch every app.
3. Select Open LoomAI Desk. The supported Console-to-Desk sign-in flow can reuse
   your linked identity, but Desk still checks permissions independently. A
   bookmarked link is navigation, not authorization.
4. Ask each agent to create an account if needed, verify their email, sign in to
   Desk and register an agent profile. In Agents & access, assign their exact
   registered verified email or agent reference to this target as Support,
   Reviewer, or both. Assign yourself Support explicitly if you will reply.

Visitor and agent: continue the conversation

5. The visitor can ask the AI to speak to a person or use the exposed handover
   button. Complete the configured intake fields and any required consent.
   Anonymous continuation is available only when that deployment permits it;
   contact requirements can differ between deployments.
6. Check the transition state. Waiting for a person is not an agent reply or a
   guaranteed response time. AI orchestration is paused during pending or active
   human control; messages go through the human-support channel.
7. In Desk, the authorized agent selects Conversations, the correct target and
   waiting conversation, then Claim. Read the permitted transcript and intake
   details before replying. Human replies can reach the visitor asynchronously
   without another AI query. Check delivery status: accepted, pending and failed
   delivery are not interchangeable.
8. Use Return to AI in Desk or the visitor's permitted Continue with AI control
   for an explicit transition back. Closing a tab, signing out, inactivity or a
   network interruption does not itself authorize the AI to resume.

Closing, resolving and reviewing

Close conversation ends the runtime conversation. It does not by itself resolve
an external support request. If the context panel exposes Resolve request, that
updates the linked request only; it neither closes the conversation nor returns
control to AI. In Console integration support, reopen the request explicitly.
If its linked conversation is closed, choose Start new conversation to create
a new episode without replaying the original intake message.

Action reviews are separate from live conversations and ordinary chat confirmation.
They require review enablement, a real application-originated proposal and an
explicit Reviewer assignment. Approving a proposal is not a successful business
execution receipt; inspect the returned execution outcome.

Common issues and human help

For an empty queue, verify the app's assigned target and the agent's current
Support assignment. For delayed messages, inspect delivery state before retrying.
An outage must not silently return a human-owned conversation to AI. Ask the
integration team about unresolved access, intake, delivery or transition failures;
do not share service keys or copy another deployment's credentials.
