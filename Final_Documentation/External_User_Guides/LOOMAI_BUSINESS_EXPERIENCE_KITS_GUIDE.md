# LoomAI Business Experience Kits

## Choose Your Starting Point

Open Console and choose **AI Customer Experience** for a general branded AI
workspace, or **Dealership AI Experience** for stock discovery, comparisons and
dealership knowledge. The dealership simulator is fictional demonstration data,
not an approved production AutoTrader integration.

Both kits combine Platform and Engage. LoomAI Desk is optional; your own assigned
agents provide human support. You can request Desk during setup or add it later.

The experience cards distinguish **Adapts to your business** from **Built for
dealerships**, and show product discovery, actions and optional human support.
Their **Powered by** row identifies Platform, Engage and optional Desk.

Select a miniature screen or **Preview experience** to see **Website AI chat**,
**Max AI workspace** or **Standalone AI space**. The preview is dismissible with
the close control, Escape or the backdrop. These are illustrative examples, not
live AI or a connected support agent. Console's preview can show sample product
selection, comparisons, delivery/enquiry information and human handover without
sending a message or request. Close it to return to the same page.

Max mode is an expanded workspace layout, not another deployment or delivery
record. Website embed and standalone remain the two installable channels; you
may choose either or both during setup. Furniture and vehicle examples are
fictional marketing content, never default customer data or AI knowledge.

## Set Up The Kit

1. Sign in or create your Console workspace. Your selected kit is retained.
2. Under **Choose your starting point**, choose a **Starting experience** and
   **Test environment**. Select
   the supported AI-state storage option when the template offers a choice.
3. Set the kit name, assistant name and brand colour. Choose a standalone app,
   a website embed, or both. Supply the app address name and/or exact HTTPS
   **Website address** as requested. The preview is illustrative, not a live answer.
4. Review and select **Create my workspace**. This saves the linked setup drafts;
   your assistant is **Not live yet**. This does not deploy infrastructure,
   activate a public app, spend AI credits or assign an agent.

For **App address name**, enter only a name such as `northfield-ai`, not a full
URL. The full link updates while you type: `https://northfield-ai.apps.loomai.pro`.
Use lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens, up to 63 characters. The link
also appears in the standalone preview and review step. It is a preview, not a
live app until setup, address verification and activation are complete.

## Connect, Verify And Launch

In **My kits**, inspect each product and use its existing configuration editor:

- **Your AI assistant:** connect authorized business APIs, knowledge/storage and
  provider settings. Templates are editable starting points, not restrictions.
- **Your customer workspace:** refine branding, tools, authentication and delivery. A website
  embed and standalone app share the AI deployment but have separate installation
  records, origins and activation states. Review the full preview in Engage.
- **Human support:** enable the Desk integration for the verified deployment,
  then assign registered agents. A checked setup preference alone does not
  connect Desk or make an agent available.

Use **Technical details** for exact readiness codes and original diagnostics,
or the normal product editors for advanced configuration. The simpler wording
does not change the checks required to go live.

Publish, apply and verify through the existing deployment workflow. Connect each
app to its verified assignment, resolve the displayed readiness checks, then
explicitly activate it. Copy the website script from its installation details.
For a custom domain, add the required DNS records and complete domain verification.

Promote the tested configuration through the existing staging-to-production
workflow, then connect the apps to the verified production assignment. Kit
metadata does not introduce another publishing or promotion mechanism.

## Continue Adapting

Open **Edit your experience** for draft appearance and delivery changes. Live
apps retain the existing explicit disable/edit/activate controls. Advanced edits
remain available in the normal product editors and are not overwritten by a
stale kit form. A missing resource is shown as missing, never as a healthy app.

Use **Get setup help** when connecting your own systems. Source
permissions, credentials, document storage, DNS and human staffing are real
customer prerequisites; selecting a kit does not manufacture them.
