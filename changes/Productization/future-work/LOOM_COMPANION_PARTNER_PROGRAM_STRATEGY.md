# Loom Companion Partner Program Strategy

Status: planning document (2026-04-20)

This document defines the partner and affiliate program for Loom Companion. The goal is to build a distribution channel through integrators and agencies so the builder can stay in build mode while partners handle merchant deployment and support.

---

## 1) Why Partners

A solo developer cannot scale merchant acquisition and onboarding while also building the product. The solution is a partner channel:

- partners find merchants and close deals
- partners deploy and configure the product
- partners provide first-line merchant support
- the builder stays focused on platform and product development

This is not outsourcing. It is a distribution model where partners earn recurring revenue for bringing and maintaining merchants.

---

## 2) Partner Types

### 2.1 Shopify Agencies

Who they are:

- agencies that build and manage Shopify stores
- typically manage 10-50 active merchant clients
- already trusted by merchants for app recommendations

Why they matter:

- they have existing merchant relationships
- they can deploy Loom Companion as part of their service offering
- they can upsell from Free to Growth/Pro tiers as part of store optimization

### 2.2 E-Commerce Consultants

Who they are:

- independent consultants who advise merchants on store optimization
- focused on conversion, UX, and revenue growth
- usually manage 5-20 clients at a time

Why they matter:

- they are always looking for tools that improve client metrics
- a working AI shopping assistant is a strong deliverable
- they can position Loom Companion as part of a conversion optimization package

### 2.3 AI Integrators

Who they are:

- technical consultants or small firms specializing in AI implementation
- they help businesses adopt AI tools across their stack
- often work with multiple platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom)

Why they matter:

- they understand the technical value of the platform
- they can deploy across multiple verticals as we expand
- they become long-term partners as the product catalog grows

## 3) Commission Structure

### 3.1 Base Model

- 20% recurring commission on all paid plans
- paid monthly as long as the merchant remains active
- no cap on number of referrals

### 3.2 Revenue Per Partner

Based on Growth plan ($29/month):

- 10 merchants = $58/month passive income per partner
- 25 merchants = $145/month passive income per partner
- 50 merchants = $290/month passive income per partner

Based on Pro plan ($79/month):

- 10 merchants = $158/month passive income per partner
- 25 merchants = $395/month passive income per partner
- 50 merchants = $790/month passive income per partner

### 3.3 Incentive Tiers

Starter Partner (0-9 active merchants):

- 20% recurring commission
- standard partner support

Growth Partner (10-24 active merchants):

- 20% recurring commission
- priority support channel
- early access to new features
- co-marketing opportunities

Premium Partner (25+ active merchants):

- 25% recurring commission
- dedicated support
- influence on product roadmap
- featured in partner directory
- white-label option (when available)

## 4) Partner Readiness Checklist

Before recruiting partners, the following must be in place.

### 4.1 Must Have (Before First Partner)

1. **Working demo store**
   - a live Shopify store with Loom Companion installed and configured
   - demonstrates all tier capabilities (Free, Growth, Pro)
   - partners can show this to prospects

2. **Deployment time under 45 minutes**
   - install app from Shopify App Store
   - connect store
   - trigger initial sync
   - configure intelligence surfaces
   - verify working on storefront
   - total time: under 45 minutes for a competent integrator

3. **Partner documentation**
   - deployment guide (step-by-step with screenshots)
   - configuration reference (what each setting does)
   - troubleshooting guide (common issues and fixes)
   - merchant FAQ (answers to questions merchants will ask)
   - pricing and positioning guide (how to pitch to merchants)

4. **Referral tracking**
   - unique referral codes per partner
   - dashboard showing installs and conversions attributed to each partner
   - automated commission calculation
   - monthly payout reporting

5. **Partner communication channel**
   - dedicated Slack workspace or channel
   - response time commitment: under 4 hours during business hours
   - weekly digest of updates, fixes, and new features

6. **Partner agreement**
   - terms covering commission, payment schedule, exclusivity (none), termination
   - clear scope of partner vs. builder responsibilities
   - mutual expectations on support and response times

### 4.2 Should Have (Within First 3 Months)

7. **Partner dashboard**
   - self-service view of all referred merchants
   - real-time install and revenue tracking
   - commission history and payout status
   - merchant health indicators (active, churned, needs attention)

8. **Multi-store management**
   - partners can manage multiple merchant stores from one view
   - bulk configuration templates
   - centralized health monitoring across stores

9. **Configuration templates**
   - pre-built configurations for common store types
   - fashion, electronics, health/beauty, home goods
   - partners can deploy a working config in minutes

### 4.3 Nice to Have (Month 4-6)

10. **Partner API**
    - programmatic access for partners who want to automate deployment
    - create store connections, push configurations, check sync status

11. **White-label option**
    - partners can rebrand the intelligence surfaces
    - custom colors, logos, and widget text
    - for Premium Partners only

12. **Partner certification program**
    - short online training (video + quiz)
    - certified partners get a badge and directory listing
    - builds credibility for both partner and product

## 5) Partner Recruitment Strategy

### 5.1 Where to Find Partners

**Shopify Partner Directory**

- Shopify maintains a directory of agencies and freelancers
- filter by service type (store setup, optimization, apps)
- identify agencies that already recommend AI or chat tools

**Shopify Community Forums**

- active agencies and consultants answer merchant questions
- look for people recommending tools and providing advice
- these are natural partner candidates

**LinkedIn**

- search for "Shopify agency," "Shopify consultant," "e-commerce consultant"
- filter by follower count and activity level
- prioritize those who post about AI, automation, or conversion optimization

**E-Commerce Conferences and Meetups**

- Shopify Unite, Commerce+, local Shopify meetups
- virtual events and webinars in the Shopify ecosystem
- these are high-trust environments for partner recruitment

**Existing App Review Ecosystem**

- bloggers and YouTubers who review Shopify apps
- offer them early access and partner program membership
- their reviews drive organic installs

### 5.2 Recruitment Messaging

**Subject: Partner with Loom Companion - AI Shopping Intelligence for Shopify**

Hi [Name],

I noticed you work with Shopify merchants on [specific thing they do - store optimization, app recommendations, conversion improvement].

I built Loom Companion, an AI shopping intelligence layer for Shopify stores. It adds smart product insights, AI-powered search, and contextual assistance directly into product pages and the storefront - not just a chat bubble.

I am building a partner program for agencies and consultants who want to offer this to their clients. The structure:

- 20% recurring commission on all paid plans ($29-$79/month per merchant)
- deployment takes under 45 minutes
- you get a dedicated support channel and early feature access
- I handle all product development and infrastructure

If you manage Shopify stores and want to add an AI capability to your service offering, I would like to set up a 15-minute call to show you how it works.

Best,
[Name]

### 5.3 Recruitment Timeline

**Week 1-2: Prepare**

- finalize demo store
- write partner documentation
- set up referral tracking
- create partner agreement
- set up partner Slack channel

**Week 3-4: Seed Partners (3-5)**

- reach out to 20-30 potential partners
- target: 3-5 signed partners
- these are the founding partners who shape the program
- give them extra attention and gather feedback

**Month 2: Refine**

- adjust documentation based on partner feedback
- fix any deployment friction they surface
- improve configuration templates
- target: 5-10 total active partners

**Month 3-4: Scale**

- open partner program more broadly
- create partner landing page
- target: 15-25 active partners
- each deploying to 3-5 merchants

**Month 5-6: Mature**

- launch partner dashboard
- introduce tiered incentives
- target: 25-50 active partners
- target: 100-250 merchants through partner channel

## 6) Partner Onboarding Flow

### 6.1 Application

- partner fills out a short form (name, company, number of Shopify clients, services offered)
- no gatekeeping in early phase - accept most applicants
- auto-generate referral code on approval

### 6.2 Kickoff Call (15 minutes)

- live demo of the product
- walk through deployment flow
- answer questions
- share documentation and referral code

### 6.3 First Deployment (Assisted)

- partner deploys to their first merchant with builder available on Slack
- builder watches for friction and documentation gaps
- fix issues in real time
- this first deployment should be smooth - it sets the tone

### 6.4 Independent Deployment

- partner deploys subsequent merchants independently
- uses documentation and configuration templates
- contacts support only for edge cases

### 6.5 Ongoing Relationship

- weekly updates in partner Slack channel
- monthly check-in for Growth and Premium partners
- feedback loop: partner surfaces merchant needs, builder prioritizes

## 7) Partner Support Model

### 7.1 Support Tiers

**Tier 1: Partner handles**

- merchant onboarding questions
- basic configuration (appearance, placement, content)
- "how do I" questions from merchants
- billing questions

**Tier 2: Builder handles**

- technical deployment issues
- sync failures or data quality problems
- bugs and unexpected behavior
- feature requests and roadmap questions
- infrastructure and platform issues

### 7.2 Response Time Commitments

- Partner Slack channel: under 4 hours during business hours
- Critical issues (product down, sync broken): under 1 hour
- Feature requests: acknowledged within 24 hours, prioritized weekly

### 7.3 Escalation Path

Partner identifies issue -> posts in Slack channel -> builder triages -> fix or workaround within committed SLA

## 9) Builder Time Allocation

The entire point of the partner program is to protect build time.

### 9.1 Target Allocation

- 85-90% building (platform, features, new products)
- 5-10% partner support (Slack, documentation, calls)
- 5% program management (onboarding, commission, reporting)

### 9.2 Time Boundaries

- partner Slack: check 2-3 times per day, not continuously
- partner calls: maximum 3 hours per week
- documentation updates: batch weekly
- commission and reporting: automate as much as possible

### 9.3 Scaling Without Scaling Support

As partner count grows beyond what one person can support:

- better documentation reduces repeat questions
- configuration templates reduce deployment friction
- partner FAQ and knowledge base reduce Slack volume
- senior partners mentor newer partners
- eventually: promote top partners to "partner leads" who handle onboarding

## 10) Partner Agreement Key Terms

### 10.1 Commission

- 20% recurring on all paid plans referred by partner
- paid monthly, 30 days after merchant payment clears
- commission continues as long as merchant remains active and paying

### 10.2 Attribution

- referral tracked by unique partner code
- 90-day attribution window (merchant installs within 90 days of first click)
- last-touch attribution (if multiple partners involved, last referrer gets credit)

### 10.3 Exclusivity

- non-exclusive: partners can recommend competing products
- no territory restrictions
- no minimum deployment requirements

### 10.4 Termination

- either party can terminate with 30 days notice
- partner continues to receive commission on existing merchants for 6 months after termination
- no clawback on earned commissions

### 10.5 Responsibilities

Builder responsibilities:

- maintain and improve the product
- provide partner support within committed SLAs
- pay commissions on time
- give advance notice of pricing or program changes

Partner responsibilities:

- represent the product accurately
- provide first-line support to their merchants
- not make unauthorized promises about features or pricing
- follow brand guidelines when promoting the product

## 11) Metrics to Track

### 11.1 Partner Health

- number of active partners (deployed at least 1 merchant in last 60 days)
- average merchants per partner
- partner churn rate
- time from partner signup to first merchant deployment

### 11.2 Channel Performance

- total merchants through partner channel vs. organic
- revenue through partner channel vs. organic
- merchant retention rate by channel (partner vs. organic)
- average revenue per merchant by channel

### 11.3 Program Efficiency

- builder hours per partner per month
- support tickets per partner per month
- documentation coverage (% of partner questions answered by docs)
- time to deploy (getting faster over time)

## 12) When to Start

Do not start recruiting partners until:

1. the product is live on the Shopify App Store
2. at least 5-10 organic installs have validated the deployment flow
3. deployment time is confirmed under 45 minutes
4. basic documentation exists
5. referral tracking works

Estimated readiness: 2-4 weeks after Shopify App Store approval

Start with 3-5 founding partners. Learn from them. Then scale.
