# AI Fabric Framework: Strategic Vision & Company Roadmap

AI Fabric Framework is positioned to become the **infrastructure layer for the next generation of AI-native applications**. We enable any existing application to gain intelligent capabilities without rebuilding from scratch.

**The Vision**: Every app will have a "MAX AI Mode" - and we provide the foundation to build it.

## Product Strategy

### Three Products, One Platform

#### 1. AI Fabric Framework (Open Source Core)
**Target**: Developers building AI features
**Pricing**: Free / Open Source
**Purpose**: Build community, drive adoption, create ecosystem

**What's Included**:
- Core RAG orchestration
- ONNX embeddings (free, local)
- Lucene vector storage (embedded)
- Basic action execution
- Entity sync via annotations

**Value**: Zero cost entry. Proves the concept.

#### 2. AI Fabric Cloud (Managed Platform)
**Target**: Companies wanting to move fast
**Pricing**: Usage-based + seat licenses

**What's Included**:
- Managed vector databases (Milvus/Qdrant)
- Multi-tenant isolation
- Pre-built action library
- Dashboard for configuration
- Analytics and monitoring
- Auto-scaling

**Pricing Tiers**:
| Tier | Queries/Month | Price | Target |

| Growth | 100K | $499 | Growing SaaS |
| Scale | 1M | $2,499 | Established |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Custom | Large orgs |

#### 3. AI Fabric Marketplace
**Target**: App owners who want ready solutions
**Pricing**: Revenue share (70/30)

**What's Included**:

**Example Marketplace Items**:
